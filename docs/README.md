## Ethical Design Guidelines for Track Your Triggers

### Purpose and scope
The app is strictly designed as a tool for self-observation and self-discovery. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, or provide psychological assessments.
User data is not collected or used for research purposes, targeted advertising, or purposes unrelated to the functionality of the app.

### Data minimization
The app only collects personal data that is necessary for its functionality. Data is not collected simply because it may be interesting or potentially useful in the future.

### User autonomy
Users decide for themselves which information they choose to record. Users can view, edit, and delete their data at their own discretion.

### No unsupported conclusions
The app may present patterns and correlations observed in a user's own data, but it does not present these patterns as psychological diagnoses or causal relationships.
The app does not speculate about why a user experiences certain emotions or triggers.

### Privacy by design
Privacy and data protection are considered and integrated into the application's architecture and design from the beginning, rather than being added as an afterthought.
